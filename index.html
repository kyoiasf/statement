Do androids dream of electric sheep?

What decision have you made for yourself in this game of beginnings ? How do I prove that I exist? Do words have these fixed meanings? How do you get out ? Where do we determine where to focus our consciousness? Why ? What lies beyond words??

I work with sounds that cannot be classified as instruments in order to uncover and explore themes of loneliness, belonging, and the hard to find, tuning into the more subtle sounds. I'm inspired by philosophical ideas surrounding technology, magic and neofuturism. I use Ableton to compose music and am also interested in audio engineering which can be found in my music using max/msp, and touchdesigner for more graphic visuals that deal with interactive art, visualization for storyboards and mood design. I'm also involved in using textual elements such as poetry, and playing with ideas of encoding and de-ciphering. My work often focuses on creating immersive environments where sound, visuals and text become emotional spaces rather than fixed compositions. Through experimentation with digital tools and unconventional audio sources, I aim to create experiences that feel deeply human. I'm interested in how technology can transform ordinary noises or footage into something emotionally resonant. Textual elements within my pieces help with anchoring a form of memory that slips between our daily lives and give snippets of what once was. I aim to explore emotions with questions, typographic play and compositions, giving a new sense of perception for the viewer each time they see it again. The idea of a "loop" is one of the main things that drive my practice, as I continue to play with sounds, text and the "unknown", barriers within language, barriers within understanding what we know of, barriers within memory. I'm interested in the tension between overwhelming visuals and text, map-like structures where the way is decided by the perception of the viewer.
